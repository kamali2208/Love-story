A Letter From
Kamali 💚

Dear my Mamaaa,

Enna sollanum nu theriyala mama... but indha letter type panna start pannumbodhe enakku romba emotions varudhu.

Namma first meet romba simple-aa irundhaalum, andha small moment en life-la ivlo periya memory-aa maarum nu appo enakku theriyala.

First pesinadhu, first call, WhatsApp-la "Hi, naan Thraveen" nu sonnadhu, naan "Hi, naan Kamali" nu sonnadhu... ellame ippo kooda en mind-la clear-aa irukku.

Konjam konjam-aa pesinom. Konjam konjam-aa close aanom. And one day namma rendu perum namma feelings-a realise pannom.

Mama, nee enakku lover mattum illa. Nee en comfort, en safe place, en best friend, en happiness.

Un call varumbodhu oru happiness. Un message varumbodhu oru smile. Un kooda pesumbodhu enakku world konjam peaceful-aa feel aagum.

Neraya sanda varalam. Neraya misunderstandings varalam. Aana adhellam vida namma rendu peroda bond dhaan enakku mukkiyam.

Naan life-la enna nadandhaalum, un kooda memories create pannitu, un kooda travel pannitu, un kooda sirichitu, un kooda old aaganum nu aasai.

Nee happy-aa irukkanum mama. Nee aasapadra ellame un life-la nadakkanum. Un dreams ellame true aaganum.

And one thing... naan unna vida maaten. Life full-ah un kooda irukkanum nu en heart-la oru aasai irukku.

Forever yours,
Your Ammu — Kamali 💚♾️

Happy Birthday My Mamaaa 🎂💚

Enakku vera edhuvum venam mama... nee en kooda irundha podhum.

Namma life-la innum neraya birthdays, neraya memories, neraya laughs, neraya hugs and countless beautiful moments together create pannanum.

Unakku health, happiness, success, peace and nee aasapadura ellame kedaikkanum.

Nee en life-la vandhadhukku apram naan romba happy aana. Un call and un message enna happy-aa feel pannum.

Nee enakku lover mattum illa. You are my comfort, my safe place, my favourite person and my best friend.

Life full-ah un kooda travel pannanum. Un kooda memories create pannanum. Un kooda happy-aa irukkanum.

Naan eppovume unna en life-oda precious part-ah ninaippen mama. ♾️💚

Happy Birthday once again, Thraveen. 🎂💚 Stay blessed and be happy forever.

Forever yours,
Kamali — Ammu 💚♾️
