A Letter To
My Mama

My dearest Mama,

Sometimes I think about how a normal day can quietly become the beginning of something that changes our whole life.

Unkooda pesura every small conversation-um enakku romba precious. Sometimes we laugh, sometimes we fight, and sometimes oru word kooda pesaama irundhaalum your presence itself gives me comfort.

Naan perfect person illa mama. Enakku mistakes irukkum. Aana one thing mattum true — I genuinely care about you and I genuinely want you in my future.

Un smile paatha enakku automatically happiness varum. Un message varumbodhu even a normal day becomes a little more special.

Namma relationship perfect-aa irukkanum nu naan expect pannala. Good days or difficult days, we should learn, understand and choose each other again and again.

Mama, thank you for becoming someone I can trust, someone I can talk to, someone I can miss and someone I can love with my whole heart.

With all my heart,
Ammu 💚

Happy Birthday My Mamaaa 💚

Innaiku un birthday mama... but enakku idhu just oru birthday illa. Indha world-ku nee vandha special day.

Namma story-la irukkura every small moment-um enakku precious. Un call, un message, un smile, un voice — everything makes my day special.

Mama, un life-la nee aasapadura ellame unakku kedaikkanum. Un dreams successful aaganum. Unakku happiness, peace, success and beautiful moments always irukkanum.

Namma future-la innum neraya birthdays, neraya trips, neraya laughs, neraya photos, neraya hugs and countless memories create pannanum.

Nee enakku lover mattum illa mama. Nee en comfort, en safe place, en favourite person and en best friend.

Life full-ah un kooda travel pannanum. Good days-laum difficult days-laum, happy moments-laum silent moments-laum — I want us to keep choosing each other.

Happy Birthday once again, Thraveen. 🎂💚

Forever yours,
Ammu 💚♾️
