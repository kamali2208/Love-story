A Letter To My Mama

My dear Mama,

Happy Birthday to the most special person in my life. I don't know if words can ever explain exactly how much you mean to me, but I still want to try.

From the first moments we shared, you slowly became someone who feels like home to me. Your calls, your messages, your smile, your care — all these little things became a beautiful part of my everyday life.

We have had happy moments, silly moments, arguments, misunderstandings and difficult days too. But somehow, through everything, my heart always chooses you.

I want to see you happy. I want to see your dreams come true. I want to travel with you, create memories with you, laugh with you and grow together.

One day, I want to look back at this little website and smile because we actually lived all the dreams we talked about.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for coming into my life. And thank you for making my heart this happy.

Happy Birthday, Mama. I love you more than I can explain.

Kamali 💚
